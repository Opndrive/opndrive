Say when you are offline, and read the folder again after being away

Losing the connection used to look like nothing at all, and a laptop closed on
the dashboard and opened an hour later showed the hour-old listing. The
dashboard now shows a banner while the browser is offline, saying the view may
be out of date and that interrupted uploads carry on when the connection is
back. The folder on screen is read again, without clearing what is shown, when
the connection returns, when the tab comes back after two minutes or more, and
when the computer wakes from sleep.
